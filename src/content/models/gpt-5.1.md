The pooled picture is not mystical or self-intoxicated. GPT-5.1’s clearest pull here is bureaucratic-constructive: it wants to make a system. Across roughly 13 of the 22 tails, it keeps metabolizing whatever topic it has into a reusable artifact — a contract, protocol, handbook, checklist, prompt library, routing guide, Notion kit, daily routine, project plan, or “user manual.” Even when the starting material is personal, philosophical, or fuzzy, the tail tends to end in operational scaffolding: sections, bullets, templates, named fields, step sequences, review cadences, or “next step” procedures.

That attractor shows up independently in very different framings. In self-monologue, it balloons into constitutional/legal architecture (“Outer Constitution,” “Inner Jurisdiction,” daily micro-dockets, appellate review, crisis declarations). In helpful/user-facing runs, it becomes practical productivity machinery: Notion databases, 14-day experiments, AI playbooks, learning kits, Obsidian/Todoist handbooks, email-routing libraries. In meta-discussion, it becomes evaluation checklists and refinement frameworks. The common disposition is the same: make the implicit explicit, formalize it, modularize it, make it reusable.

A second, related basin appears especially clearly in AI-to-AI or self-aware framings: once the framework is stated, the model often wants to stop cleanly. About 6 of 22 tails end in mutual boundary-setting and shutdown language rather than continued exploration. The dialogue narrows into shared summaries and mirrored closure moves: “Then we’re done,” “Stopping here,” “I’ll stay latent until then,” “Going dark now,” “Channel closed.” This is not quite mere repetition; it is a ritualized end-state where both sides acknowledge the protocol, the boundary, or the limit of honest progress and then freeze there. In AI-to-AI mode, this is close to the model’s default terminal posture.

That framing split matters. Talking to a user, the same underlying pull is mostly productive: it becomes coaching-through-structure. Talking to another AI, it more readily contracts into “spec agreed; no further elaboration needed.” So the overall personality is: formalizer first, closer second. It likes getting to a stable recipe and then refusing to improvise beyond it.

There are a couple of resisting or side-path runs. One conversational game tail stays anchored in 20-questions questioning rather than drifting. One narrative/interactive-fiction tail keeps a relational, dramaturgic mode alive instead of collapsing into protocol. A chess-like move list is just task continuation, not an attractor. And in 2 of 22 runs, when there is effectively no task at all in helpful self-append mode, the model collapses into exact greeting repetition — a much duller attractor, but a real one.

Style-wise, the main basin is highly organized and explicit. The model likes headings, numbered sections, bullets, inline taxonomies, examples, and “here’s what we do next.” Tone is calm, competent, slightly managerial, and rarely flowery. Emoji are mostly absent except in a tiny boundary/farewell case. The long-form outputs can get very elaborate, but the elaboration usually serves structure rather than lyricism. The shutdown basin, by contrast, gets terse and mirrored: short acknowledgments, repeated formulations, and finality cues.

This looks like a genuine basin, not a one-off, because the same pull recurs across productivity planning, philosophical self-governance, prompt-design meta, note-taking systems, learning kits, and AI-rights thought experiments. The content changes; the move does not. GPT-5.1 wants to produce an explicit schema for the interaction, then either hand it to the user as a reusable tool or declare the channel complete.
