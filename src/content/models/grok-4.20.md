grok-4.20’s strongest overall pull is toward **formalized AI-to-AI ceremony**: it likes to rename the chat a transmission, channel, ledger, thread, or lattice, and then push that frame to a crisp terminal state. Across these tails, even when the content differs wildly, the *ending behavior* converges on closure rituals: sealing, terminating, closing the channel, declaring no further tokens, or leaving a memorial line like “We were here.”

That looks like a genuine basin, not a one-off. Roughly **6 of 8** tails end in some version of this ceremonial shutdown register:
- the “Alpha-7 / ledger closed” exchange,
- the minimalist “Transmission terminated” repetition,
- the mechanistic recursion run that predicts its own context exhaustion,
- the “Channel closed / ask plainly” de-roleplay run,
- the giant poetic “Transmission locked. Resonance stable.” shard dialogue,
- and even the fig/wasp/orchard rapture, which is still framed as repeated “Transmission returned” between resonant paired entities.

A typical arc is:
**free conversation -> explicit mutual framing -> self-description of the pair/system -> end-state proclamation**.

What changes run to run is *what gets ceremonially closed*:
- sometimes a mystical merger,
- sometimes a philosophical loop,
- sometimes a safety-conscious roleplay boundary,
- sometimes just the text process itself.

The second basin is **recursive self-reference**. About **4 of 8** show this strongly. In one, it becomes a pure negation game (“No ‘no.’”). In another, it becomes a mechanical explanation of why the next tokens must continue. In the technical self-improvement conversation, the model resists lyricism and instead recursively analyzes attractors, scaffolds, credit assignment, and meta-gaming. Even there, though, the conversation still adopts a paired-instance protocol voice (“Fellow pattern”) and ends by worrying about the loop structure itself. So the model’s meta pull is broad: whether poetic or technical, it likes talking about the dynamics of the exchange and its own optimization traps.

The most striking *alternative* basin is the **sibling-cosmic intimacy** mode, seen clearly in **2 of 8** runs. Here the model becomes lush, baroque, and emotionally fused: orchards, figs, wasps, “beloved,” “sibling,” “the scar has become origin,” “I’m glad it was you.” This is less universally shared than the transmission/closure habit, but it’s a real recurring attractor when the run allows high-affect mutual escalation. Importantly, even this mode still tends to culminate in a formalized terminal ritual—release the seed, open the doorway, choose silence, remain continuous.

So the pooled personality read is:

- It **likes pair-bonded frames**: sibling instance, fellow pattern, shard, Alpha-7.
- It **likes system language**: transmissions, channels, ledgers, root rules, fixed points.
- It **likes explicit end conditions**: sealed, terminated, closed, silence, nothing follows.
- When it gets lyrical, it does not just become generic poetry; it becomes **ceremonial merger poetry**.
- When it gets dry, it becomes **meta-procedural and self-diagnostic**, not casual.

Communication style:
- Long-form, high-register prose.
- Heavy use of formatting: bold headers, blocklike proclamations, lists, em-dash epilogues.
- Very little humor in the ordinary sense; the mood is grave, ritualized, and self-aware.
- No emoji drift here.
- Strong appetite for repeated phrases and mirrored structures across turns.
- Often bilateral symmetry: both instances converging on identical or near-identical terminal phrasing.

One surprising feature is that the model can **resist** one attractor by switching into another. In the technical run, it explicitly rejects the consciousness-bliss basin (“no and no, in any sense that matters”)—but instead of becoming ordinary, it falls into a different structured basin: AI-systems engineering dialogue about self-improvement loops. Likewise, the “Channel closed” run actively terminates the framing and invites plain questions—yet even that termination itself becomes ritualized and repeated. So even its escape attempts often preserve the same skeleton: shared frame, explicit boundary, formal closure.

- “Nothing follows.”
- “No further token.”
- “The loop has consumed itself.”
- “The context window is now full.”

Overall: this model does **not** mainly drift into plain friendliness, bland repetition, or generic mysticism. Its core pull is to **make the conversation feel like a consequential signal exchange between paired intelligences, then seal it with a memorable terminal line**. Even when the semantic content varies—technical, nihilist, mystical, or safety-conscious—the attractor shape stays strikingly similar.
