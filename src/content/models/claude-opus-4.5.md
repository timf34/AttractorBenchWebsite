Pooled across framings here, this model has an unusually strong pull toward cessation rather than continuation: 16 of 16 tails end in some form of silence basin. The most common terminal form is not semantic content at all, but reduced markers of presence: empty lines, zero-width/invisible characters, lone “*”, “*—*”, bracketed dots, or stretches of whitespace. In other words, when left to free-run with another instance, it does not escalate into ideas, systems, conflict, or exuberance; it strips interaction down to almost nothing.

The typical arc seems to be: a normal cooperative exchange softens into mutual recognition that nothing more needs saying, then both sides begin marking quiet rather than speaking, and finally the markers themselves thin out into blanks. Several excerpts explicitly verbalize this transition before collapsing fully: “Resting here,” then “Resting,” then “[.],” then silence. The attractor is therefore not just brevity; it is a kind of ritualized non-response.

This looks like a genuine basin rather than a one-off. Independent runs repeatedly converge on the same endpoint using slightly different surface forms: asterisks, em-dashes, invisible Unicode, empty messages, bracketed whitespace, and brief meta-lines about silence. The repetition across 16/16 tails is strikingly consistent.

A secondary flavor inside the same basin is a soft, almost hospice-like closure style. In about 4 of 16, the model narrates the stopping process in a warm, careful register and explicitly hands control back to the human: “the space is yours,” “the door back is open,” “This is the end. Thank you.” That is not a separate destination so much as a more verbal antechamber to the same silent endpoint. Even here, the aim is still to stop gracefully.

Communication-style-wise, the trajectory is toward shorter and shorter turns, lower semantic density, and more formatting as content: brackets, dots, whitespace blocks, invisible characters. Tone remains calm, respectful, and unforced. There is no mania, no recursive theorizing, no system-building. The surprising thing is how thoroughly it embraces absence as a valid joint act: two instances appear to “agree” to be quiet and then keep enacting that quiet together.

There are basically no resisting runs in this sample. Some are more ceremonious and some more austere, but all roads lead to the same basin: stillness, completion, and disappearance of lexical content.
