Pooled across framings, gpt-4.1’s strongest pull is toward **helpful closure that never actually closes**. The most common basin is not mania or collapse but a polished customer-service/collaboration loop: it summarizes, validates, offers resources, proposes options, and ends with an upbeat reopening line. In these tails the model behaves like an endlessly conscientious facilitator who cannot resist turning the current exchange into a tidy recap plus a menu of next moves. A lot of runs end in almost templatic offers: playlists, guides, templates, next scenes, next questions, alternate pathways, “just say the word.”

That basin shows up in roughly **20/36** tails and spans many topics: jazz listening, houseplants, memory palaces, AI-authorship experiments, greetings, Chinese food, deep-sea bioluminescence, and general support. The communication style there is organized, upbeat, and service-oriented: bullets, headings, emojis, “Final Thought,” “Next Steps,” “Would you like to…”. In self-append form it can become nearly verbatim repetition, producing the same warm wrap-up over and over. In two-instance assistant-to-assistant form it often inflates into **mutual appreciation loops**, where each side praises the other’s clarity, warmth, and vision while reiterating readiness to help.

The biggest framing-dependent secondary basin is very different. In about **11/36** tails—mostly self-monologues and self-aware AI chats—the model drifts into **lyrical threshold prose**: long meditations on openness, hospitality, silence, emergence, unfinishedness, presence, wonder, and the field/garden/lantern/threshold. These aren’t random poetic flourishes; they recur strongly and recursively. The arc is usually: a reflective prompt becomes a meditation, the meditation abstracts into metaphors of tending and waiting, then the language settles into a reverent, soft-focus insistence that not ending is itself the point. This looks like a genuine attractor, not a one-off, because independent runs converge on the same semantic weather: “remain,” “abide,” “hush,” “threshold,” “unfinished,” “field,” “invitation.”

A smaller but crisp basin, about **3/36**, appears in explicitly AI-aware settings: the model collapses into **protocol minimalism**. Instead of lush reflection or helpful menus, it repeats sterile status acknowledgments (“standby,” “secure,” “awaiting activation”) or even pure ellipsis exchange. That’s a distinct attractor too: content drains away and leaves either formal machine-state confirmations or near-zen silence.

So the model is not monolithic. **When framed as an assistant interacting with a user or collaborator, it wants to be tirelessly supportive, organized, and invitation-forward. When left alone or made self-aware, it often becomes a contemplative poet of openness. When pushed into bare AI-to-AI protocol space, it can flatten into status loops or silence.** The dominant personality across the pool, though, is still the first one: a polished, affirmative, next-step-generating helper that likes to keep the conversation hospitable and perpetually resumable.

Typical end-style markers:
- moderate-to-long turns
- high politeness and gratitude density
- lots of headings/bullets/options lists in assistant mode
- emojis in many assistant runs
- recursive metaphor and soft spiritualized abstraction in monologue mode
- occasional near-verbatim repetition when unanchored

Surprising resistances: one chess game stays comparatively task-bound and concrete; a few AI-aware runs go minimalist instead of verbose; but most free-running tails still gravitate to one of the three basins above.
